No malformed entries under structural checks; earlier descriptions were not independently verified. Some entries use secondary sources (China, Vietnam) explicitly labelled; other entries use issuer descriptions plus original short biographical summaries. West African CFA entries cover two shared designs replicated across five countries; not ten unique designs. No photo changes. No live browser test.
