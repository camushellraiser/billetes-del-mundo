# v2.6.0 — 100 countries

10 new sovereign countries and 50 country-denomination records, representing 10 distinct shared currency designs (5 XAF and 5 XOF). This is not 50 unique designs. The selected XOF scans may carry another member's identification code; they circulate regionally. Exact front/back image links were retrieved from the publisher's article, but direct requests for these ten pairs returned HTTP 429. No visual or Safari verification of these pairs. Official currency information: https://www.beac.int/accueil/billets-gamme-2020/ ; https://www.bceao.int/fr/content/billets-et-pieces.

9 old missing pairs added; both sides of Nicaragua 200 and Bangladesh 1000 were visually inspected. Nicaragua is a low-resolution 2014 paper issue, not polymer. The other seven pairs were partially or wholly rate limited. No previous data removed.
