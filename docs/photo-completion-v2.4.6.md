## Limitations
- The 0.50 quetzal is retained because Banco de Guatemala expressly states legal tender under article 26: https://www.banguat.gob.gt/page/preguntas-frecuentes-sobre-el-nuevo-billete-de-q20000. A secondary source differs; the available image is low-resolution.
- Guatemala 5, 10 and 20 use earlier printings of the continuing ordinary designs. Guatemala 100 commemorative 2026 and Paraguay 100000 new design 2026 are source-listed; direct fetch was rate limited. The other five Paraguayan denominations retain the prior circulating family. The Ethiopian 5-birr note is an older issue.
- Global browser loading, latest printing of every denomination, and reproduction terms are not comprehensively audited. No actual Pages/Safari test performed.
